## Link quality rules

1. Prefer a small number of relevant Kenyan editorial links over large numbers of generic directory links.
2. Ask for descriptive, natural link text such as “Dala Festival 2026 event guide” or “Ferré Gola in Nairobi”.
3. Do not insist on exact-match anchor text.
4. Do not pay for followed links or participate in link exchanges.
5. Never add claims of independent operation, organiser authorisation, official status or exclusive ticketing status without the user's prior approval of the exact wording.
6. Do not direct website visitors to competing ticket sellers.
